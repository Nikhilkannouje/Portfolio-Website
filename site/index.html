LLM · Document automation

AI Invoice & PO Matching

Reads invoice PDFs, checks them against purchase orders and explains every mismatch before anyone approves a payment.

Task
Matching invoices to POs by hand is slow and error-prone; a wrong price or currency can reach payment.
Input →
Invoice PDF + purchase order
Output →
Field-level match, confidence score, anomaly flags and a plain-language explanation
Method
Gemini extracts the data; deterministic rules decide the match, so the result stays auditable.
Role
Built the extraction pipeline, matching and scoring logic, and the SAPUI5 approve/reject flow with mock SAP posting.
Stack
SAP CAP · SAPUI5 · Node.js · Gemini API

Visual AI · Product design

CMF Variant Generator

Try colour, material and finish options on a product render without repainting every variant by hand.

Task
CMF exploration repeats the same masking and recolouring for every option.
v0 · built
Click a region (colour flood fill), apply a colour and finish that keeps the original shading, compare six variants. Pixels outside the selection are never changed.
Next
Segment Anything masks; inpainting for real material textures (leather, fabric, brushed metal).
Limits
v0 is classical image processing, not AI. Finishes are visual approximations, not material simulation.
Stack
JavaScript · Canvas · planned: SAM, diffusion inpainting

03 · Approach

How I work.

The same four steps, whether the input is an invoice, an image or a designer's week.

  1. 01 · Understand

    Find the slow step.

    Pin down what is repeated, who does it and what goes wrong.

    ↳ invoice-to-PO field checklist
  2. 02 · Measure

    Size the problem.

    Collect time and friction before choosing a solution.

    ↳ friction survey template
  3. 03 · Build

    Simplest reliable model.

    Use AI where input is messy; keep decisions in rules people can audit.

    ↳ LLM extraction + rule-based match
  4. 04 · Label

    Show the limits.

    Say what is built, what is a prototype and what is sample data.

    ↳ “No AI model in v0” tag

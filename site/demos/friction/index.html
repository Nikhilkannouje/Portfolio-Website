Method and limitations.

  • Participants: 5–10 designers or design students, recruited directly. Each logs the tasks that took the most time in a typical week.
  • Measures: self-reported minutes per week, times per week, friction on a 1–5 scale, and whether the task feels repetitive.
  • Limitations: a small convenience sample; self-reported time is approximate; results describe this group only and don't generalise to all designers.
  • Ethics: participants are anonymised by ID, and notes are shared only with consent.
